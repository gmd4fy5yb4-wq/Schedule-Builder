Getting started.
Most admins get a full season scheduled in one sitting. Here is the path:
1
Name your league
Pick your sport and FieldDay adapts its language to match — softball, basketball, soccer, whatever you run. You get a 6-character league code that identifies everything you build.
2
Add divisions and teams
Set up your divisions, then add teams by hand or import a whole roster from a CSV in one go.
3
Set up fields and umpires
Tell FieldDay where games happen and who officiates. The scheduler uses both to avoid conflicts for you.
4
Generate the schedule
One click builds a balanced season. Adjust anything by hand afterward — snapshots let you undo, so nothing you try is permanent.
5
Share it with your coaches
Send a read-only link. Coaches and parents see the schedule and standings on their phones without creating an account. Scores you enter update standings instantly.
